**Design Movement:** Industrial Field Tool / Utilitarian Craft
**Core Principles:** Speed of use in the field, zero cognitive load, big tap targets, instant feedback
**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy background with electric cyan accents — evokes clean water, professionalism, and trust. Warm amber for totals/money to draw the eye.
**Layout Paradigm:** Single-scroll vertical card stack — each step is a full-width card the thumb can reach. No sidebars, no tabs. Progressive disclosure: pane count → services → plan → total.
**Signature Elements:** Large numeric pane counter with +/- buttons; animated total that updates live; a "receipt-style" summary card at the bottom.
**Interaction Philosophy:** Every action gives immediate tactile feedback. Tap a service toggle and the total animates. No page transitions — everything lives on one screen.
**Animation:** Subtle number roll-up on total changes; card entrance with slight upward slide; toggle switches with spring physics.
**Typography System:** `Space Grotesk` (bold, geometric) for numbers and headings; `Inter` for labels and body. Numbers are always large and legible.
